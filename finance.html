A private household system with public decision tools

Finance

Most finance software tells you what category a purchase landed in. This project is meant to explain the harder things: what the household actually spends, how investments are growing, what a house truly costs after equity and opportunity cost, and which accounts still make sense if retirement comes early.

The preview below is deliberately fictional. The real dashboard, account links, transactions, and household assumptions are never included in the public response and remain behind the same passkey login used across my private apps.
